2024 October 7-10 Kiosk Lake Loop from Access Point 29 (Kiosk)
4 days, 3 nights. 60.5km total distance, 15.2km total portage, longest portage ~2.9km from Three Mile Lake to Manitou Lake
Morning launch on day 1 from Kiosk. Rainy day all day but at least it wasn't too cold. Kiosk is a nice big lake, and it was very quiet.
Pretty typical portage representative of the entire trip. Clearly this is a well used route, both currently and historically. Lots of leaves hiding rocks however.
Route through the weeds/swamp from Club Lake to the portage to Mouse Lake on Day 1. First of several areas on the map that warned of low water late in the season, didn't have to drag the canoe once however (lots of poleing tho).
Relaxing at the end of Day 1 on Mouse Lake. None of the sites were phenomenal. Was supposed to stay on Club Lake but went ahead anyways since it was supposed to be empty and didn't want to start the next day doing the low water thing described above.
Overcast morning on Day 2. Cold and windy day overall, but at least not much rain.
Paddling up Erables Lake. The wind was a bit of an issue, blowing straight at me most of the day, but I took my time.
The sun came out for my campsite on Maple Lake. Looking back towards the central part of the lake, nice island site in the middle of the lake in view, however too windy there and wanted a good start on day 3 the next day.
Early morning mist and moon on Ratrap Lake. What an interesting name for a lake.
Sunny and cold on day 3. Three Mile Lake was nice. The night went down to 0 I'm pretty sure based on the ice on the tent poles.
Lunch stop on Manitou Lake. By far the nicest looking lake on the loop. Weather was participating and not too windy today.
Looking at the island site at the north end of Manitou Lake on Day 3. Stayed here tonight, once of the nicer sites I've had for sure. Nobody on the lake.
Scenic hammock spot.
Sunset and fire from my raised campsite, extremely relaxing and lots of wood for a good fire.
Day 4 heading home bright and early from Manitou. Launched from my site around 645am, very nice paddle with the wind with me the entire day. Got home in good time. Great trip overall, would repeat the loop 100%.
