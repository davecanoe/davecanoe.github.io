2023 September 25-28 - Cedar-Carl Wilson-Mink-Cedar from Access Point 27 (Brent).
Day 1 - Brent to Carl Wilson (via Bug, Ironwood), Day 2 - Carl Wilson to Mink, Day 3 - Mink to Laurel, Day 4 - Laurel to Brent
Total Portages - 8 (4430 m), Longest Portage - 1070 m
Total Distance 61 km, Distance Paddled 56.5 km
Average Distance per day ~15.25 km
Paddling up Cedar Lake on Day 1, large stone fireplace on this site, likely from an old cottage. Slightly overcast first day but calm weather.
Looking out at Carl Wilson lake after the 3 portages. The first one from Cedar-Bug lake was VERY up-hill, but was able to one-carry it with a slight rest still. The other two portages were quite easy as they were either downhill or flat with a nice wide path. Carl Wilson is a nice lake.
Some nice fall colours on the small stream leading to the 1070 portage out of Carl Wilson to Little Cauchon. Had a nice site on Carl Wilson, good spot for a hammock and a sandy bottom for a bit of swimming.
Looking out at Little Cauchon lake, quite windy on Day 2 but the wind was with me so I made great time going across the Cauchon lakes. Nice and sunny as well, not too many people out.
The fall colours were a bit muted due to all the pine trees, but some nice spots of colour (Cauchon Lake).
Paddled about 1/3rd of the way up Mink Lake, took the best of the first 5 sites I found, nothing too great but good enough. I'll have to explore more of Mink Lake soon, possibly from Kiosk. Beautiful misty morning on Day 3.
A calm sunset and moon rising on Laurel lake on Day 3, checked out all the sites and this was by far the best one, spent a lot of time reading and relaxing in the hammock. Heard a lot of loons on this trip and saw almost nobody on Day 3, just a few canoes passing through on the other side of Laurel in the afternoon. The wind was against me most of Day 3, but not as strong as on Day 2, so it took a bit longer but got to explore the north side of the Cauchon Lakes.
Very misty start on Day 4, could barely see the shoreline just infront of me on Laurel. A bit cool out, paddled half the day with the touque on, but again some perfect weather.
Heard a few motorboats on Aura Lee in the night, some ok looking sites but nothing exciting. Was a nice paddle through the continued mist.
Arriving back on Cedar lake on the morning of Day 4, the mist is still quite present and I didn't really have to deal with the sun until around 10am. The wind had calmed down today so it was nice not to have to fight it back on the big lake.
One last look back at the fall colours with mist on the north-west portion of Cedar lake. A great trip overall with lots of nice weather, couldn't have planned it any better.
