2022 September 10-11 - Sec Lake from Access Point 20.
Day 1 - Access to Campsite (circled in red)
Day 2 - Campsite to Access
This was Gordon's first canoe camping trip, so we made it easy and fun
Gordon arrived at the furthest campsite on the lake (and most isolated). It had a bit of a rock wall to it from the lake, but it was nice and big up top and Gordon really seemed to enjoy it. This is him as he arrived and I was paddling around to find an easy spot to bring up the Canoe.
To make it fun, we had lots of snacks, here he is relaxing, eating some chips. We learned that next time, we should bring at least 2 bags of chips.
More snacks....
Playing with some trucks in the dirt while we have a fire going. We were trying to warm up a bit after our swim. Gordon really seems to like the backcountry camping, so I'm hoping we can keep that enthusiasm alive for a few more years at least.
Nice sunrise on the 2nd day, chilly morning but we made pancakes and Gordon slept well in the tent.
Pancake breakfast, Gordon was happy I didn't forget the syrup (and that bears didn't take our food bag).
Heading back home to the car, Gordon has his pizza and headlamp. Once we arrived back, we decided to go for 2nd breakfast at the Irving big stop for more pancakes. Great trip overall.
