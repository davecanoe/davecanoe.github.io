2026 May 3-5 Grand Lake Loop from Access Point 22 (Achray)
3 days, 2 nights. 40km total distance, 12km total portage, longest portage ~6.9km from Carcajou Lake/Creek to Clemow over the hydroline road. My longest portage to date.
Earliest I've set out in the year, launch was very cool temperature wise but wind wasn't too bad. Wore my touque and stayed sunny most of the day. Mostly followed the shore towards Carcajou bay due to the freezing water temps.
In Carcajou Falls just past Carcajou Bay I caught this little brook trout. Casting into the waterfall runoff and letting it push my lure out and slowly reeling back in with a spinner. Lots of fun but I didn't fish here for long.
Carcajou creek heading into Lower Spectacle Lake is pretty wide and looked nice with the spring colours with the sun. Saw some people camping on the spectacle lakes, clearly out for the fishing.
Nothing too special with the spectacle lakes, each lake wasn't very big and the sites looked ok but not memorable. Easy to get to however.
Little Carcajou Lake has only one site so I took it. Full day to get here with the portages but a nice evening with a good hammock spot. Had a nice fire at the site with lots of dry wood available. Wind was the least the first day as compared to the next two days.
Cabin on Wenda Lake. This location also hosts a 3.5k portage accross to Grand as well as a campsite. Lots of people at the campsite still sleeping as I paddled by.
It was a bit windy and I had a lot more travelling to do otherwise I would've liked to explore the south arm of this lake. Overall not that spectacular but quite peaceful out. Stopped on a site to have a quick lunch.
Start of the big portage, which is a series of portages. This is before the branch off to Greenleaf Lake, but a nice preview of the hill I'll have to climb over to get to Grand/Clemow.
No description needed.
The portage itself was pretty wide and well maintained as it was a road for the most part, the uphill portions were killer however.
Uphill portion behind me, looking down the hydroline towards Grand Lake. The low spot way in front is where Grand Lake meets up with the road. Another 800m to the left is the protage along the same road to Clemow.
