2024 September 28-30 Carcajou Bay from Access point 22 (Achray). 3 days, 2 nights. Family trip with the Bonds, The Barge's first trip.
Setting out from the launch on Grand Lake. Nice looking day, kids are excited and The Barge is loaded up properly for the first time, did not pack light..
Having a fire on Day 1, everyone is really excited and getting along well, not the best site on the bay but safest for the kids. We actually day paddled here before and had a picnic on this site.
A short trip down the bay and across a portage to High Falls on Carcajou Bay. Really nice to paddle here and explore the falls, water was quite chilly in this area.
Nice lunch spot on an unofficial camp site next to the falls, did a quick dip as the water was quite cold, nice and sunny though. You can see the same falls from the site.
Katie paddling back from across the bay with the kids on Day 2. They went to go paint and draw, I collected firewood and explored. The weather was quite amazing the whole time.
Everyone hanging out at the site, a little crowded but everyone was doing well and got along great, the kids had a blast digging in the roots.
View across the bay towards the two sites on the other side.
Kids all hanging out near the area they spent all weekend digging at, maybe sped up erosion a bit but they had a ton of fun and all did really well.
The barge at the Achray Beach on Day 3.
A really good first family trip with the new canoe and with friends. Would 100% go again, everyone had a good time and we all had a nice dip at the beach. Two nice looking 18'+ boats.
