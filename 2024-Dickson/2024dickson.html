2024 September 15-18 Dickson Lake Loop from Access Point 17 (Shall Lake)
4 days, 3 nights. 60k total distance, 21k total portage, longest portage ~5k from Dickson-Round Island Lake
Shirley lake on day 1 just after the portage. Really hot day, nice looking lake but the trailer on the "paddle-in" campsite kind of took away from it.
The view after entering onto McKaskill Lake. The upper part of the lake is down and to the left of the photo. Portages on the first day were not too bad, a bit up and down. The portage leading into McKaskill is the last time I saw anyone else until 3 days later when I arrived on Booths Lake.
Sunrise just after setting off from my island site on McKaskill. Not the greatest site but not bad either. Two pine martens kept me up at 1am having a very loud argument in the trees directly above my tent.
The view looking down Animoosh lake from the campsite on the portage. Really, it was a nice big site with good views, but just not the best place to stop on the trip for me. Wind was blowing down the lake a bit so it took a bit longer to paddle accross to the portage.
Hammock location on my site on Dickson Lake. The site itself was ok, but good views out into the souther part of Dickson. There is still a lot of Dickson to expore, so I'm sure I'll have to return and head up to White Partridge soon. Windy day on the lake, did a bit of swimming of the site but not a great entry for me or the boat.
Sunset on Dickson.
Sunrise on Dickson looking back towards Cisco Bay while paddling across to the big portage.
Like a little cave opening in the trees, this is the view paddling up to the 5k portage to Round Island lake.
Self-explanatory.
Despite being 5k, this really was a nice and scenic portage. A bit of a hill to climb at the beginning, but after that it was a nice walk in the forest. Crossed two very well maintained logging roads along the way. It took about 2.5hrs to finish the portage, and the strategy here was to just take more breaks and double-carry the whole way rather than single-carrying the canoe or pack and doubling back.
View on Round Island lake just after setting off from the portage, much of this part of the lake is shallow with huge boulders underwater, hit a few of them for sure.
Campsite on Round Island had a nice rocky point with good views of most of the lake. The firepit is a bit exposed and the only good place to hang the hammock also happened to be the only decent tent pad. The other two sites on theis part of the lake were pretty rough looking. Overall, I wasn't impressed really with any of the sites on this trip.
Clear morning with the moon still up when I was just about ready to launch. This was the longest day as far as travel goes so wanted to start early. Lots of little portages and little crappy lakes along the way until Booth.
View looking onto Booth lake from the portage. The day was quite clear and warm, and the water on Booth was relatively calm. This view is looking down the lake towards Tattler Lake. One portage of note today was Marshy to Presto lake, it had a really slippery start on wet boulders, and had a creek crossing in the middle that you had to paddle. Did a lot of cursing on that portage. Overall it was a good trip, pushed my solo boundaries a bit more, but also felt I haven't reached the limit in days or distance/day yet. Jumpy as always was a great companion.
