2017 October 3-5, 2 night trip.
Day 1: Kingscote-Big Rock-Byers-Branch-Scorch Lake
Day 2: Scorch-Branch-Byers-York River-High Falls Pond-High Falls
Day 3: High Falls-High Falls Pond-York River-Byers-Big Rock-Kingscote.
We were staying at Katie's cottage so we decided to do an Algonquin trip from the south since we probably wouldn't be near this part of the park for a while. We had just bought our first canoe, "Jumpy", and were excited to take it out for a big trip! Kingscote access was about an hour from the cottage, and on the dirt road in a large logging truck was on my ass the whole drive in. We were also trying out our new cherry paddles for the first real trip.
Kingscote is a nice big lake with some cottages on it. It was a nice calm day when we left, the lake wasn't nearly this smooth on our return.
Katie decided to flex her muscles on this portage and try out hauling the canoe, they both look good here.
After getting over the fact that we just gave our canoe it's first scratches from us, we made it to Scorch lake. Not the best swimming at this point in the year but it was a nice sunny trip here.
Katie stoking the fire.

Day 2 was full of rain, this was earlier in the day before it really started, I'm wearing my "sperm hoodie" that I took to NZ.
High Falls site looking back the way we came in. This is early on Day 3 with the sun coming up. When we arrived on Day 2 it was raining so hard we couldn't even set up for a few hours, we just stood in our rain gear and waited for the rain to subside. Eventually we got the tent set up but it took a while to get the inside all dried out. I don't think we ate dinner until it was almost dark. As a side note, this is the most southern site in Algonquin Park.
Stopped at a site on Byers lake for lunch on Day 3, the colours on the hills were a little past peak but still very pretty. Katie is pumping water. Overall a really good first trip.
